FBT SECURES CONTRACT TO RESTORE HEIGHWAY THEATRE

16 October 2026 | Project News

Forge Building Team has been appointed to undertake structural restoration works at the Heighway Theatre in Eastbourne following the recent incident that caused extensive damage to the venue.

The project will focus on repairing the main entrance and front of house areas, with the aim of restoring the historic venue and supporting its eventual reopening.
FBT will work alongside specialist contractors to help bring this important cultural landmark back into use.

We’re excited to announce the addition of a new staff car park at the rear of the Forge Building Team site. The expansion will make parking more accessible and ease congestion during busy periods.

As part of the works, we’ll be relocating some of the existing air conditioning units to create space for the new layout. The project also includes upgraded lighting, CCTV coverage, and improved pedestrian routes to ensure safety and security remain at the heart of the design.
