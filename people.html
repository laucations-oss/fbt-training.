Zachary Willis

Managing Director | Forge Building Team

Zachary Willis is the founder and Managing Director of Forge Building Team (FBT), bringing over 30 years of experience in the construction and metalwork industry.

Originally from South East London, Zachary began his career working on construction sites before progressing into project management. His experience and passion for the industry eventually led him to establish Forge Building Team, which has since grown into a successful business employing around 70 people.

Under Zachary's leadership, FBT has developed a strong reputation for quality workmanship, reliability and delivering specialist metalwork projects across London and the South East. He remains actively involved in the business, maintaining close relationships with clients and taking a personal interest in the projects undertaken by his team.

Outside of work, Zachary enjoys spending time with his wife Molly and their dog Ziggy. A keen traveller, he also enjoys good food, exploring new places and spending time with family and friends.

Pictured: Zachary with his wife Molly and their dog Ziggy outside their family home.

Sharon Keele

Operations Director | Forge Building Team

Sharon Keele is the Operations Director at Forge Building Team, overseeing the day to day running of the business and ensuring projects are delivered efficiently, safely and to the highest standards.

With over 20 years of experience in operations and project management, Sharon has developed a strong background in coordinating teams, managing resources and maintaining client relationships. Since joining FBT, she has played a key role in supporting the company's growth and developing its operational processes.

Sharon works closely with Managing Director Zachary Willis and the wider management team, helping to coordinate everything from initial client enquiries and project planning through to the delivery of completed works.

Outside of work, Sharon is currently enjoying the challenge of renovating a property in Norfolk, splitting her time between the countryside and London. When she's not busy with building work, Sharon enjoys the outdoors, long country walks and exploring the Norfolk coastline.
