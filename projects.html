Explore a selection of our recent work, showcasing our expertise in design, fabrication and construction.

01 / THE MERIDIAN BUSINESS CENTRE

FBT was appointed to deliver structural steelwork and bespoke architectural metalwork for the Meridian Business Centre, a prestigious commercial development in the heart of London.

Working alongside the principal contractor, our design, fabrication and installation teams supported the project from initial planning through to completion.

The development features modern office accommodation, shared meeting facilities and contemporary communal spaces, designed to meet the needs of a busy commercial environment.

02 / NORTHBRIDGE DATA CENTRE

FBT was appointed to deliver specialist structural steelwork and architectural metalwork for the development of the Northbridge Data Centre, a major investment in the region's digital infrastructure.

Working alongside the principal contractor and specialist engineering partners, our teams supported the project from initial fabrication through to final installation.

Zachary Willis Visits the Completed Northbridge Data Centre

FBT Managing Director Zachary Willis visits the completed Northbridge Data Centre, a project in which he played a leading role in developing several of the facility's key design features.

Working closely with the wider project team, Zachary helped shape a facility that combines operational efficiency, security and modern infrastructure, reflecting FBT's commitment to delivering high-quality, purpose-built environments.
